R-014 Ranges a project states over its homes
When: a project shows the span of its homes
Then: display.price_range, display.fee_range, display.living_space_range,
display.rooms_range and display.plot_range are "min – max unit" with a spaced en dash
and the unit once at the end ("2 000 000 – 5 000 000 kr", "2 – 4 rum", "62 – 118
kvm"); equal bounds → the one value; only a minimum → "från min unit"; only a maximum
→ "upp till max unit". The fee range is per month ("kr/mån"). Zero counts as missing.
CRMs: all
Examples: price_range 2000000 to 5000000 → "2 000 000 – 5 000 000 kr"
