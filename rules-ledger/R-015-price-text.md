R-015 Price wording (drafted 2026-09-24, awaiting Patric's validation, question 90)
When: the office's own price wording is shown next to the price ("Utgångspris", "Accepterat pris")
Then: display.price_text is price_text as the office wrote it with its first letter in upper
case: "utgångspris" → "Utgångspris". Nothing when the office wrote none.
Applies: display.price_text (price_text)
CRMs: all
Examples: "utgångspris" → "Utgångspris"; "Accepterat pris" → "Accepterat pris"; null → nothing
