R-006 Elevator
When: the elevator is shown
Then: display.elevator is "Ja" when elevator is true and "Nej" when false, and the
elevator_description follows after a comma when there is one. Not stated (null) →
nothing.
CRMs: all
Examples: true, "Hiss från markplan" → "Ja, Hiss från markplan"; false → "Nej"
