R-002 Areas
When: a size in square metres is shown
Then: the number per R-001, a hard space, "kvm". display.living_space from living_space;
display.additional_space from additional_space; display.building_area from
building_area; display.plot_area from plot.area. display.area joins living space and
additional space: "82 kvm + 12 kvm biarea"; only living space → "82 kvm"; only
additional space → "12 kvm biarea"; neither → nothing. Zero counts as missing.
CRMs: all
Examples: 82 and 12 → "82 kvm + 12 kvm biarea"; 45.5 and null → "45,5 kvm"
