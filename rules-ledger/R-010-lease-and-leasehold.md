R-010 Lease and leasehold
When: a site leasehold (tomträtt) or a lease (arrende) is shown
Then: display.leasehold is leasehold.fee per R-001 and "/år", and when leasehold.term is set
" (löper till <date>)" with the date as YYYY-MM-DD in Swedish time. display.lease the
same from lease. No fee → nothing.
CRMs: all
Examples: fee 6646, term 2029-12-31 → "6 646 kr/år (löper till 2029-12-31)"
