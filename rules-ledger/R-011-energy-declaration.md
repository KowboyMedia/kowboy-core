R-011 Energy declaration
When: the first building carries an energy declaration
Then: display.energy_declaration is the status label as the CRM sends it followed by the
date it was performed (YYYY-MM-DD, Swedish time) when there is one: "Utförd
2021-02-26", "Behövs ej". display.energy_class is the class letter as sent.
CRMs: all
Examples: Performed 2021-02-26 class C → "Utförd 2021-02-26" and "C"
