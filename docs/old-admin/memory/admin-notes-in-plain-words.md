On 2026-10-06 12:15Z Patric read the card note under "Offices Vitec lists" and said of the sentence
"Reading the groups takes the login's rights to Vitec's CRM, with the CRM password when one is
typed; without them every office is used. While offices are typed under "Offices it may see",
those are used instead.": "This description doesnt make sense at all". It packed three facts into
clauses with pronouns ("them", "those") whose referents a cold reader cannot find.

**Why:** admin text is for a reader who must explain Core to a client (his 154 answer: "in simple
terms, so a cold reader can understand how the system works and instruct the client").

**How to apply:** one fact per sentence, the subject named each time (the login, the office, the
brokerage), no pronoun that reaches back over a semicolon, no reference to a field that is not on
the page, and say the consequence in the reader's world ("Core cannot see any group and uses every
office"). Read the note aloud as the person who will phone the brokerage. See
[[ask-before-adding-to-core]] for what may be on the page at all.
